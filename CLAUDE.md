# LUNA project guide

LUNA is a browser based Moon and Mars mission trainer built with JavaScript ES modules and Three.js. Source modules live in `dist/`; `dist/game.js` and `dist/luna-playable.html` are generated by `npm run build` and must not be edited directly. Run the relevant Node tests, `npm test`, and `npm run check` after code changes.

## ASTRA, guidance, and learning

ASTRA is a local, rule based assistant. Its capability boundary is `dist/astra-tools.js`: only read, preview, and UI tools belong in the frozen registry. ASTRA must not confirm a choice, apply effects, advance procedures, spend resources or time, or write saves. Pass cloned snapshots to answer code. The player performs and confirms every physical or mission action. Route and decision previews remain non-mutating.

ASTRA replies are immediate and distinct from Earth communications, which retain their simulated light speed delay. Ground numeric replies in a current tool result and label the source as a forecast from current state, cached data with its date, or an authored assumption. Never call bundled JPL, DONKI, LOLA, or MOLA data live. Keep Moon and Mars progress separate, and keep suit and habitat inventories distinct.

Guidance levels are Coach, Advisor, and Quiet. Coach asks a guiding question before a mission answer and is the default in training and the early outpost stages. Advisor gives a direct answer. Quiet limits unsolicited advice to warnings. Opening or moving an ASTRA or tutorial panel does not complete a task. Keep panel drag, saved position, viewport clamping, and reset behavior intact. Respect reduced motion and caption preferences for notices and animations.

## Invariants

- ASTRA perceives, previews, and advises; it never acts for the player.
- Previews do not mutate state; a confirmed decision applies once.
- Procedures advance only through player input.
- Moon and Mars progress and suit and habitat inventories remain separate.
- Cached science is never described as live; teaching values are labeled assumptions.
- Earth comms are delayed; ASTRA is local and immediate.
